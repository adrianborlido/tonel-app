Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 14, 2025  ·  Last updated: October 30, 2026

Short version: Tonel needs no account and never sells or shares your personal data. Your workouts stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud account. Tonel collects only anonymous usage analytics.

Overview

Tonel ("the app", "we", "our") is a workout logging and exercise tracking app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Your workout data

Routines, workouts, personal records and custom exercises are stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled for Tonel, they also sync through your private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. This is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy; the developer cannot access this data.

Tonel works fully offline and without iCloud.

Apple Health

Saving workouts to Apple Health is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, Tonel writes finished workouts to Apple Health as strength training. Tonel never reads data from Apple Health, and Health data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

Analytics

Tonel uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage events (for example, which features are used) and a random app-instance identifier. This data:

  • is not linked to your identity;
  • does not include your workout content;
  • is not used for advertising;
  • is not used to track you across other apps or websites.

Tonel does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Data we do not collect

  • Personal information such as your name, email, age or location
  • Health data read from Apple Health
  • Payment information (purchases are handled by Apple)

Export and deletion

You can export your full workout history to CSV at any time from Settings → Export data. Deleting the app removes its local data. iCloud data can be deleted from the iOS Settings app → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children's privacy

Tonel does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your region).

Changes to this policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
