Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 14, 2025  ·  Last updated: June 14, 2025

Short version: Tonel does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Overview

Tonel ("the app", "we", "our") is a workout logging and exercise tracking app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

We are committed to your privacy. Tonel is designed to work entirely on-device, without any servers, accounts, or data collection of any kind.

Data we do not collect

Tonel does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, age, location)
  • Health or fitness data
  • Device identifiers or analytics
  • Usage statistics or crash reports sent to us
  • Payment or purchase information

Data stored on your device

All workout logs, exercise history, and settings you create in Tonel are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's built-in storage mechanisms. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it or back it up via iCloud (which is managed entirely by Apple under their own privacy policy).

Third-party services

Tonel does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data services. We do not use tracking SDKs of any kind.

iCloud backup

If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device, your Tonel data may be included in that backup. This is handled entirely by Apple. You can manage your iCloud settings in your iPhone's Settings app.

Children's privacy

Tonel does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your region). Since we collect no data at all, this policy applies equally to all users regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
