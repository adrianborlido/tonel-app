Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for people who want to get stronger, not manage software. Every screen has a job. Every tap counts.

Routines

Build unlimited workout templates with any exercises, sets, and ordering. Start a session with one tap.

Live tracking

Log every set as you go. An elapsed timer, rest countdown, and previous-session reference keep you in the zone.

Personal records

PRs are detected automatically using estimated 1RM — so a smart 5-rep set can beat a sloppy single. Badges appear the moment you break a record.

History & volume

Every session is saved with total volume, duration, and a per-exercise breakdown. See your progress at a glance.

Exercise catalog

Over 115 exercises across all muscle groups, bilingual (English and Spanish). Add custom exercises when you need something specific.

Full privacy

No account, no login, no analytics. Your workouts stay on your device. Always.

Privacy first

Your data lives on your phone. Full stop.

Tonel doesn't have servers. There's no account to create, no subscription to manage, and no analytics harvesting your health patterns. What you lift stays with you.

  • No personal data collected, ever
  • Works completely offline
  • No third-party SDKs or trackers
  • Backed up only through your own iCloud Backup

Philosophy

Designed around the gym, not the app.

01

Frictionless logging

Less time tapping, more time lifting. Previous performance is always one glance away.

02

Honest records

PRs use estimated 1RM so your progress is measured by strength, not by which rep scheme you chose that day.

03

No dark patterns

No streaks designed to guilt you, no paywall on features you already paid for, no push notifications you didn't ask for.

04

Your history, forever

Deleting an exercise or routine never erases your logged sessions. Your data is yours, permanently.
