Getting started

New to Tonel? Here's how to log your first workout.

1

Create a routine

Tap Routines and hit the + button to build a workout template with your preferred exercises and sets.

2

Start a session

Tap Start on any routine — or start an empty workout. The live workout screen tracks your sets in real time.

3

Log your sets

Enter weight and reps (or time / distance) for each set. Your previous performance is shown next to each set. Tap the checkmark to complete it and the rest timer starts automatically.

4

Finish and review

Tap Finish to see your summary — duration, volume and new PRs — then Done to save it to your history.
