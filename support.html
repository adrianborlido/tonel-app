Getting started

New to Tonel? Here's how to log your first workout.

1

Create a routine

Tap Routines and hit the + button to build a workout template with your preferred exercises and sets.

2

Start a session

Open any routine and tap Start Workout. The live workout screen tracks your sets in real time.

3

Log your sets

Enter weight and reps (or time / distance) for each set. Tonel auto-fills your previous performance as a reference.

4

Finish and review

Tap Done to save the session. Volume, personal records, and history update instantly — all stored locally on your device.
