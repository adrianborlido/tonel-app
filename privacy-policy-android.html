Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 10, 2026

Short version: Tonel for Android needs no account, has no internet access and collects no data. Your workouts stay on your device and, if Android backup is on, in your own Google account backup.

Overview

Tonel ("the app", "we", "our") is a workout logging and exercise tracking app. This Privacy Policy covers Tonel for Android and explains what information the app handles and how. Tonel for iPhone has its own privacy policy.

Your workout data

Routines, workouts, personal records and custom exercises are stored only on your device. Tonel for Android does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere: there is no account, no sync and no server. The developer cannot access your data.

Android backup

If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include Tonel's data in your device backup, stored in your own Google account, and restore it when you set up a new device. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy; the developer cannot access these backups.

Health Connect

Saving workouts to Health Connect is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, Tonel asks for permission to write exercise sessions and then writes each finished workout to Health Connect as a strength training session (its name, start time and end time). Tonel never reads data from Health Connect, and this data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties by Tonel. You can revoke the permission or delete these entries at any time in the Health Connect app.

Analytics and advertising

Tonel for Android contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs. It does not use the Android advertising ID.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the running workout and alert you when a rest ends
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep the workout timer and rest countdown accurate while the screen is off
  • Vibration: for the end-of-rest alert
  • Health Connect (write exercise): only if you turn on saving workouts to Health Connect

Data we do not collect

  • Personal information such as your name, email, age or location
  • Health data read from Health Connect
  • Usage analytics or device identifiers
  • Payment information

Export and deletion

You can export your full workout history to CSV at any time from Settings → Export data. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from Android Settings → Apps → Tonel → Storage, removes its local data. Backups can be managed or deleted from your Google account's device backups. Workouts saved to Health Connect can be deleted in the Health Connect app.

Children's privacy

Tonel does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your region).

Changes to this policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the Google Play release notes.
